Ten year vision

Future goals.

My future goal is to study engineering and keep building skills in science, technology, and problem solving. I am especially interested in chemical or materials engineering because I like the idea of creating things that can actually be useful in real life.

Vision

Building a future with skill and impact.

Right now, I am still exploring what kind of engineer I want to become, but I know I want my future to involve science and innovation. I am interested in pharmaceuticals because it is exciting to see how chemistry, materials, and technology can be used to help people.

Taking ICS also showed me that coding connects with almost every field. Even if I do not become a software engineer, logical thinking can help me in engineering, research, and future projects.

Goals

🎓 Education

I want to keep improving in math, science, and ICS so I can be ready for engineering after high school.

My goal is to build a strong foundation now, because university level courses will need stronger study habits and deeper understanding.

🧪 Career

I am interested in chemical or materials engineering because it connects science with real products and real problems.

Pharmaceuticals also interest me because they show how engineering and chemistry can improve health and make a difference in people’s lives.

🛠️ Skills

I want to keep improving my coding, communication, problem solving, and critical thinking skills.

These skills matter because engineering is not just about memorizing formulas. It is about understanding problems and finding practical solutions.

💪 Character

Moving from Bangladesh to Canada taught me resilience because I had to adjust to a completely different environment.

I want to keep building that mindset so I can handle difficult courses, mistakes, and challenges without giving up.

🌍 Community

I want my future work to be useful to other people, not just something I do for myself.

Whether I work in technology, materials, or pharmaceuticals, I want to contribute to projects that have a positive impact.

🚀 Personal growth

I am still learning who I want to become, but I know I want to keep improving and trying new things.

This portfolio shows that process: learning from challenges, building skills, and preparing for the future step by step.
