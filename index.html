What this portfolio is about

A clear look at my growth.

This portfolio is a mix of my story, what I learned in ICS, and where I want to go next. I chose to take ICS because I have always liked computers and gaming, and I wanted to understand more about what happens behind the screen.

Even though I am still learning, this course helped me get better at breaking problems down, writing code, and not giving up when something does not work right away. It also connects to my future goals because I want to study engineering, especially chemical or materials engineering, and I am interested in fields like pharmaceuticals where science can actually help people.
